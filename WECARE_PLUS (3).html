About

About WECARE PLUS

WECARE PLUS is a healthcare service discovery platform designed to help users quickly locate hospitals, clinics, diagnostic centers, blood banks, pharmacies and emergency services. Designed as a BCA 2nd Year UI/UX academic project.

Roadside Emergency

National Highway Emergency Services

Quick access to highway assistance while travelling in India.

NHAI Highway Helpline1033 • Toll-free highway assistance

Report highway accidents, breakdowns and request assistance.
National Emergency112 • Emergency response

For urgent situations requiring emergency assistance.
NHAI states that road users can dial 1033 for emergency or non-emergency assistance on National Highways. Assistance may include ambulance, patrol vehicle and crane services depending on the highway arrangement.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about using the WECARE PLUS prototype.

How do I find a nearby hospital?

Use the header search or Find Services section, select Hospital, set your distance and apply filters.

Can I book an appointment online?

Yes. Select a specialist in the appointment section, choose a date and consultation mode, then confirm the demo booking.

Can I check hospital bed availability?

The prototype includes a sample bed availability dashboard. A production version would connect it to verified hospital inventory data.

How can I receive lab reports?

Use the Online Lab Reports section with your report ID. The current version demonstrates the interface only.

Is the AI assistant a doctor?

No. The assistant is a service-navigation demo and does not diagnose conditions or replace a qualified healthcare professional.

How can I contact support on WhatsApp?

Use the WhatsApp Connect option to open a WhatsApp chat with the configured support number.
